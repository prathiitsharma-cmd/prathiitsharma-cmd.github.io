About

I'm a third-year mechanical engineering student at the Indian Institute of Petroleum and Energy, Visakhapatnam.

My work sits where three things meet. I like the physical problem — how a propellant manifold distributes flow, or where a launcher's structure buckles. I like building the model that answers it, in MATLAB or Python, and sweeping it until the governing case falls out. And I like the business question at the end: what should we actually do, what does it cost, and how sure are we?

Outside the lab I'm the founding president of 180 Degrees Consulting IIPE, where our analysts do pro-bono strategy work for NGOs.
